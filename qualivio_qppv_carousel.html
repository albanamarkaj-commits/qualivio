01 · The Role

What is a QPPV?

The Qualified Person Responsible for Pharmacovigilance is the named individual that a Marketing Authorisation Holder (MAH) must appoint to establish and maintain its pharmacovigilance system across the EEA.
  • Personally responsible for the safety profile of every authorised product.
  • Must be continuously available, around the clock.

02 · The Legal Basis

Where the rules
come from

Directive 2001/83/EC Article 104: the core obligation to appoint a QPPV
Regulation (EC) No 726/2004 Pharmacovigilance duties for centrally authorised products
Directive 2010/84/EU and Reg. (EU) No 1235/2010 The 2012 EU pharmacovigilance reforms, now consolidated into the texts above
Implementing Reg. (EU) No 520/2012 Minimum requirements for the QPPV, expanded in EMA GVP Module I

03 · Location & Status

Where must the
QPPV reside?

  • Must reside and operate in the EU/EEA.
  • Can be a direct employee or a contractor, as long as they stay continuously at the MAH's disposal.
  • The UK needs its own arrangement: a UK QPPV, or a UK LPPV where the QPPV sits outside the UK.

04 · Core Duties

What the
QPPV oversees

  • Establishing and maintaining the PV system and its quality system.
  • Full oversight of product safety profiles and any emerging concerns.
  • Signal detection activities and Risk Management Plans (RMPs).

05 · The Single Contact

One point of
contact for
the authorities

  • Acts as the single contact for regulators, available 24/7.
  • Ensures complete, accurate, and timely responses to safety requests.
  • Holds the authority to influence the quality system and PV activities.

06 · The PSMF

The Master File
connection

The Pharmacovigilance System Master File (PSMF) describes how the whole system works.
  • The QPPV keeps the PSMF current and accurate.
  • Authorities can request it at any time.

07 · Delegation

Delegate tasks,
keep the
accountability

  • Specific tasks can go to trained, qualified staff.
  • Overall oversight and accountability stay with the QPPV.
  • Many MAHs nominate a back-up person to cover the QPPV's absences.
