Question 01 · Minimum criteria

How do you handle a case that does not meet the minimum reporting criteria?

  • Start with the four criteria: an identifiable patient, an identifiable reporter, a suspect drug, and an adverse event.
  • If one is missing, pursue follow up and document every attempt you make to obtain it.

Question 02 · SUSAR on a deadline

What would you do if a SUSAR appears close to the submission deadline?

  • Trigger an immediate medical review.
  • Apply the expedited timelines (7 or 15 days).
  • Escalate through the defined process.
  • Always choose compliance over convenience.

Question 05 · Missed timeline

What happens if a regulatory reporting timeline is missed?

  • Document the deviation immediately.
  • Run a root cause analysis.
  • Implement CAPA.
  • Focus on preventing recurrence.
Never reduce it to “just apologise”.
